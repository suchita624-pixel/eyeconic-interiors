Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 2 BHK or 3 BHK flat interior cost in Patna?
An essential interior package for a 2 BHK in Patna usually ranges from ₹3.5 Lakhs to ₹5.5 Lakhs, while a premium 3 BHK turnkey setup with acrylic kitchen, sliding wardrobes, false ceiling, and PVC/wood flooring ranges between ₹6.5 Lakhs to ₹11 Lakhs depending upon material choices.
Do you provide 3D visual designs before work begins?
Yes! We provide complete 3D visualization and mood boards of your living room, kitchen, and bedrooms so you can see exactly how colors, lighting, and textures will look before off-site fabrication begins.
What is your timeline for delivering a complete flat interior?
Our standard handover timeline is 45 business days from 3D layout approval. We manufacture modular carcasses in precision factory setups to ensure zero delays during on-site assembly in Patna.
Where is your design studio located in Patna?
